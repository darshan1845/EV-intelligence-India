About the Project

EV Charging Station Intelligence Platform

About the Platform

The EV Charging Station Intelligence Platform is a data-driven system designed to analyze electric vehicle usage, battery behavior, and charging infrastructure. The platform uses Machine Learning, Data Analytics, and Visualization to generate useful insights for EV users and charging infrastructure planning.

The system analyzes EV-related datasets and provides predictions through multiple Machine Learning models. It also supports charging station requirement analysis to help identify areas where charging infrastructure may need further attention.

Project Objectives

  • Predict EV energy consumption.
  • Estimate battery health.
  • Identify range anxiety risk.
  • Analyze charging station requirements.
  • Analyze EV charging infrastructure.
  • Provide data-driven insights for EV infrastructure planning.

Machine Learning Models

  • Energy Consumption Prediction - Predicts the energy consumed by an EV based on vehicle, battery, distance, charging and environmental features.
    Accuracy: 81.05%
  • Battery Health Prediction - Estimates the health percentage of an EV battery.
    Accuracy: 95.08%
  • Range Anxiety Prediction - Predicts the risk of range anxiety based on EV usage, battery and charging-related characteristics.
    Accuracy: 97.25%
  • Charging Station Requirement - Analyzes EV and infrastructure data to support charging station planning.
    Accuracy: 78.16%

Data Sources

The project uses cleaned and processed EV usage, electric vehicle statistics, and charging station datasets. These datasets are used for Machine Learning, infrastructure analysis, and dashboard development.

System Workflow

  1. Collect EV and charging infrastructure data.
  2. Clean and preprocess the datasets.
  3. Perform exploratory data analysis.
  4. Engineer relevant features.
  5. Train and evaluate Machine Learning models.
  6. Save trained models for prediction.
  7. Integrate the models with Flask.
  8. Display predictions and insights through the web application.
