About the EV Intelligence Platform

A data-driven platform combining Machine Learning, analytics and visualization to understand EV behavior, battery performance and charging infrastructure.

01
THE PLATFORM

Built to Understand Electric Mobility

The EV Charging Station Intelligence Platform is a data-driven system designed to analyze electric vehicle usage, battery behavior, and charging infrastructure.

The platform combines Machine Learning, Data Analytics and Visualization to generate useful insights for EV users and charging infrastructure planning.

Multiple Machine Learning models analyze EV-related datasets and provide predictions for energy consumption, battery health, range anxiety and charging infrastructure requirements.
